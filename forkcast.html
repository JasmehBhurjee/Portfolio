Never miss what's actually good on campus.

Forkcast is a community for Ohio State students to find and review the food worth knowing about, especially the limited time stuff that sells out before most people even hear about it.

The problem

01

Limited edition items and one off food drops show up on campus all the time, and by the time you hear about them, they're already gone.

02

Finding out depends on who you know. If you don't have the right friends or the right group chat, you just miss it.

03

There's no shared place for students to actually review campus food and flag what's worth trying before it's gone.

What Forkcast does

Reviews, from students

Rate and review food from dining halls, food trucks, and campus spots, so good finds don't depend on word of mouth alone.

Limited time alerts

Get notified when something limited or one off shows up nearby, so you can actually try it before it's gone.

Built for one campus

Focused on Ohio State specifically, so the reviews and alerts are always about places you can actually walk to.
